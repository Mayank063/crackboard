About this data

where it comes from, and its limits

Where the data comes from

CrackBoard's problem data is built from a public, open-source dataset (liquidslr/leetcode-company-wise-problems on GitHub), which itself compiles LeetCode's own "company tags" feature — a Premium-only feature where users report which companies asked them which questions in real interviews.

What "frequency" means

The frequency percentage shown next to each problem is a relative measure of how often that problem was reported for that company, compared to other problems in the same list — not a guarantee it will appear in your interview, and not an absolute probability.

Known limitations

  • Data is self-reported by users, not verified by LeetCode or the companies themselves.
  • We don't have an exact timestamp for when the underlying dataset was last refreshed — company interview patterns can shift over time.
  • Only the top 12 most-frequent problems per company are tracked here, not the full historical list.
  • 168 well-known companies are included out of 471+ available in the source dataset, filtered for relevance.

Your progress

By default, solved status and streaks are stored only in your browser (localStorage) — clearing your browser data will reset it. If you sign in (top right), your progress also syncs to your account, so it carries over across devices and you'll appear on the Leaderboard.
